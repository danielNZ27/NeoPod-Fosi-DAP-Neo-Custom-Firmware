Qt and PySide sources:
https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/?h=v6.10.2
https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtbase.git/?h=v6.10.2

The GUI and flashing-engine Python sources, exact dependency requirements and
EXE build script are included. To rebuild with modified Qt/PySide libraries,
prepare a Python environment with those libraries and PyInstaller, then run
tools/build_flash_neo.py. The build script imports the unchanged flashing engine
into the EXE. Modification and debugging of the bundled libraries are permitted
under their respective open-source licenses.

The EXE also includes the Python runtime (Python Software Foundation and
contributors; its license is included) and the PyInstaller bootloader
(PyInstaller contributors; GPLv2-or-later with the bootloader distribution
exception). PyInstaller source and exception:
