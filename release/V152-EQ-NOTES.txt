NeoPod V152 - portrait Custom EQ and wheel controls

Install DP3_V152.IMG using your usual NeoPod update procedure.
V151 users must update the firmware AND neo_scan.bin; the four audio decoders
are unchanged from V151. The matching companion files are included under sd-card/.echo; copy sd-card
contents to the microSD root, merging .echo and preserving existing settings,
library and cover files. This release retains V151's tiny-block FLAC fix.

Custom EQ:
- All five sliders, frequencies and gain values fit the 240x320 Neo screen.
- Turn the wheel to choose a band; selection wraps through all five bands.
- Press OK to adjust the selected band.
- Turn the wheel to raise/lower its gain, limited to -12..+12 dB.
- Press OK again to confirm and save; the wheel chooses bands again.
- Adjustments apply live when the equalizer is enabled.
- Use the existing Back gesture to leave the screen.
